🎙️ Verify a Recitation How it works

Checking engine…

1

Record or upload

Use your microphone or drop an audio/video file — MP3, WAV, MP4, WebM and more. Files are processed in memory and deleted immediately.

2

AI transcribes

A Quran-specialized Arabic speech model converts the recitation to text, handling long recordings in overlapping chunks.

3

Evidence verified

The transcript is matched against all 6,236 ayat. Every result carries a confidence label — verified, good, possible, or clearly marked as incomplete evidence.

Honest by design

VERIFIED TEXT MATCH

Text, coverage and sequence thresholds all satisfied.

GOOD / POSSIBLE MATCH

Strong or partial textual evidence — shown with its exact score.

SEQUENCE RECOVERY

An ayah inferred between two strong matches — always labelled as incomplete audio evidence, never silently promoted.

Quran Shazam is text-evidence based. It does not claim acoustic proof of tajweed, makharij, or voice correctness, and it refuses to guess when the corpus is unhealthy — verification is blocked, not faked.
